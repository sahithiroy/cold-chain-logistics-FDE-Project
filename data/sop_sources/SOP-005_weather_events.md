# SOP-005: Extreme Weather Handling

**Document ID:** SOP-005
**Owner:** Dispatch Operations
**Applies to:** All dispatchers and drivers

## 1. Purpose
Extreme heat, storms, and snow increase the risk of temperature excursions and delays. This SOP explains how to respond.

## 2. Heatwaves (Outside Temperature Above 35°C)
- Reefer units work harder in heat. Check the cargo temperature of every perishable shipment in the area **every 30 minutes** instead of every hour.
- Avoid door openings. Deliveries in heatwaves should be made at loading docks with shade or cold rooms.
- If a fresh perishable shipment is already above 6°C during a heatwave, contact the driver to check the reefer settings **before** it breaches 8°C.

## 3. Storms, Heavy Rain, and Snow
- If the weather severity score (`weather_condition_severity`) is above **0.8**, the driver may stop at a safe location. Driver safety comes first.
- Check that the reefer unit has enough fuel for the extra waiting time.
- Treat expected delays of more than 4 hours on perishables as a **Tier 2 warning** (see SOP-002).

## 4. Freezing Weather (Outside Temperature Below 0°C)
- Fresh perishables can freeze. Confirm that the reefer is set to **heat mode** if the cargo drops below 2°C.
